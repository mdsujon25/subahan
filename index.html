Choose a Life with Options with Oxbridge Academy

 

Oxbridge Academy is an established distance learning college offering short learning programmes and national qualifications to students in South Africa and abroad.

The year 2017 marks two decades of Oxbridge Academy’s existence, and we’re using this opportunity to focus on providing A Life with Options for all our current and prospective students. We believe that through flexible and accessible vocational education, any individual can choose a path that enables them to pursue stimulating work that is meaningful and aligned with their interests and passions. To find out more about living a life with options with Oxbridge Academy, click here.

Affordability

At Oxbridge Academy, you can pay for your course in affordable monthly instalments. This means you can further your studies without placing yourself under unnecessary financial strain.

Accreditation

Oxbridge Academy is registered and/or accredited by a variety of regulatory bodies and professional institutes – including the DHET, UMALUSI, QCTO, ETDP SETA, and Saiosh.

Academic Support

When you study at Oxbridge Academy, you have access to personal academic support from qualified tutors who are experts in their fields.
